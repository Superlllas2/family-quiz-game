**Protocol changes (approved by Ilia on Nov 12):** `effect.params.capMs`; a new `input` kind `pass` (RaceQuestion only); the phases Attacks and TopicTie in the list of which screen each phase sends.

**Evidence (Nov 7 to 8 log):**
- **Attacks:** cleared in 2.4 to 3.3 s on average, about 60 to 80 points. Poll: "Непонятно кто на кого какие атаки использует"; "Все еще было очень быстро в моментах с бонусами. Я не успевал подумать на кого применять".
- **Race:** game 2 ended with Ilia and L1zer on the finish on the same statement; L1zer was 2 s faster over the race and lost on points ("В конце я ответил быстрее, но проиграл"). The leader won 4 of the 6 games of both nights. Simulated with 5 equal players (70 % right, finish 15, head start up to 4): −1 raises last place's chance from about 1 % to about 8 %, the lead changes from 3.0 to 3.9 per race, and the race from about 15 to about 19 statements.
- **Repeats:** all 40 race statements were used up on Nov 6; race_005, 007, 033 and 040 came up in both games. ru_places was offered in rounds 2 and 4 of game 1.
- **Closest value:** 8 of 40 guesses scored; in 4 of 8 questions nobody was within the tolerance. With 3× the tolerance 17 of 40 would have scored.
