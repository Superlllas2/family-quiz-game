# Debrief questions after a game night

A reusable template for the talk after a game night. Copy the answers into `playtests/<date>/notes.md` (step 2 of `docs/20_playtest.md`).

## What this does not ask

Two things are already measured inside the game, so the debrief does not repeat them:

- **The questions themselves** (content, difficulty, topics, facts): the star card on the phones (`fun`, `guess`, `fact`, decision 0018) and the answer log cover them.
- **The 7 poll items** on the phones after Results (`fun`, `frustrating`, `waiting`, `time`, `tvMissed`, `unfair`, `again`). The debrief follows them up instead of asking them again. Open the poll answers first and use them: "Two of you said you waited during results. When exactly?"

The mechanics of the formats (topic vote, closest value ruler and scoring, the true or false race) are gameplay, so they are in.

## How to run it

1. **Right after the poll, before anyone talks:** everyone fills in the written scorecard (part A) on their own. About 4 minutes. Paper or a form, no names. Group talk changes what people think, so it comes second.
2. **Group talk (part B), 30 to 40 minutes,** in the order of the show, so people remember the moments. You will not get through everything: ask the ★ questions first and the rest if there is energy.
3. **A few questions to one person at a time (part C),** for the players whose view matters most on a topic.
4. **Close (part D).**

Rules for asking:

- Say the intro again: "We are testing the game, not you. Problems help more than praise."
- Do not defend or explain the design. If something was unclear, that is the finding. Write it down and move on.
- Ask for moments, not opinions: "When exactly?", "What did you do then?", "Show me on the phone."
- After every answer, ask "Why?" once.
- Record audio with everyone's consent, or have the observer take notes.
- Do it the same night. By the next day people remember only the highlights.

Between game 1 and game 2 (1 minute, out loud): "Did anything break? Is there one thing we should change before the next game?" Only change things that are switches in the settings panel (power ups, targeting, music volume).

## Part A: written scorecard (on your own, RU / EN)

1. Насколько было весело в целом? / How fun was it overall? **1 to 10**
2. Насколько вероятно, что ты предложишь эту игру своей семье или друзьям? / How likely are you to suggest this game to your own family or friends? **0 to 10**
3. В следующий раз ты бы выбрал эту игру или…? / Next time, would you rather play this or…? **This game / Jackbox / Knowledge is Power / a board game / something else: ___**
4. Длина одной игры (около 10 минут) / Length of one game (about 10 minutes): **Too short / Just right / Too long**
5. Сколько игр подряд ты бы хотел сыграть? / How many games in a row would you want to play? **1 / 2 / 3 / 4+**
6. Темп между вопросами / Pace between questions: **Too slow / Just right / Too fast**
7. Понятно ли было… / Did you understand… (**Yes / Partly / No** for each)
   - а) за что дают очки и почему быстрее значит больше / how points work and why faster gives more
   - б) множители в раундах 3 и 4 (×1.5, ×2) / the multipliers in rounds 3 and 4 (×1.5, ×2)
   - в) как получить и использовать бонус / how to get and use a power up
   - г) ответ «ближе всех» (число) и как он считается / the "closest value" answer (a number) and how it is scored
   - д) почему в финале кто-то стартует впереди / why some players start ahead in the final race
8. До самого конца я чувствовал, что могу выиграть / Until the very end I felt I could win: **1 (not at all) to 5 (completely)**
9. Финальная гонка была захватывающей / The final race was exciting: **1 to 5**
10. Использовать бонусы было весело / Using power ups was fun: **1 to 5, or "didn't use one"**
11. Получать бонус от другого было весело (а не обидно) / Getting hit by a power up was fun, not annoying: **1 to 5, or "never got hit"**
12. Ведущие: я читал их реплики / The hosts: I read their lines: **Most / Some / Almost none**
13. Реплики ведущих были смешными / The host lines were funny: **1 to 5, or "didn't read them"**
14. Текст на ТВ было легко читать с моего места / TV text was easy to read from my seat: **1 to 5**
15. Телефон реагировал сразу, нажатия не терялись / The phone reacted at once, no lost taps: **1 to 5**
16. Музыка / The music: **Too loud / Just right / Too quiet / Didn't notice it**
17. Как выглядит игра (ТВ и телефон) / How the game looks (TV and phone): **1 to 5**
18. Расставь от лучшего к худшему / Rank from best (1) to worst (7): **topic vote, multiple choice, closest value, power ups, final race, hosts, results**
19. Одно слово об игре / The game in one word: **___**

## Part B: group talk, in the order of the show

### 1. Joining and the Lobby

- ★ Did anyone get stuck joining: the QR code, the name, picking a character, the language? What did you do?
- Did you know what Ready meant and when the game would start? Did the 3 s countdown feel too sudden?
- Did your character feel like "you"? Would you want something else to pick from (photo, color, voice)?

### 2. The Intro (30 s, the lights coming up, the host shots)

- ★ Did the start feel like a show beginning, or like waiting? At which point did you want it to get going?
- In the second game the intro was skipped. Did you miss it?

### 3. Topic vote (10 s)

- Did you understand that you were voting for the next topic? Did your vote feel like it mattered?
- Was 10 s too long, right, or too short? Was it fun to see what others voted for?

### 4. A question: show, answer, reveal, scores

- ★ Timer (open decision D16: 15 s for multiple choice, 20 s for closest value). Did you have time to **read** the question before choosing, or did you tap in a hurry? Was the pressure fun or stressful? Did the timer ever feel too long?
- ★ Where were you looking during a question: TV or phone? Was there a moment you needed both at once (open decision D15)?
- Did the lock-in on the phone feel sure, or did you wonder whether your answer was taken? (Pillar 3)
- When everyone had answered and the game moved on at once: did you notice, and did it feel good or rushed?
- Reveal and Scores together last 7 s. Did you see who was right, your points, and the "Why?" fact? What did you miss?
- Closest value: was the ruler on the TV clear? Did you understand why you got the points you got?
- Did the fast and the slow players feel it was fair? Did anyone feel they had no chance because others were faster?
- (If the star card was on) Did rating the question get in the way: covering the power up pick, taking your eyes off the TV, being boring after a while? Did you close it? (This asks whether the card hurts the fun, not about the questions.)

### 5. Power ups (freeze, slime, fog)

- ★ Was using a power up the best or the worst part of a round? Which one was most fun to send, and which was worst to receive?
- ★ Targeting (open decision D14): did people gang up on someone? Was that funny or did it get sour? Should it be "only hit someone ahead of you"?
- Freeze (6 taps), slime (3 blobs), fog (3 s): was each one too strong, too weak, or right? Did any of them make you miss the question entirely?
- Did you see on the TV who attacked whom ("Dad → Freeze")? Did that make the moment, or did nobody notice?
- Did you get a power up often enough? Did it feel random or earned?
- Was picking a target and a power up on the phone easy, or did it pull you away from the TV (D15)?

### 6. Rounds and multipliers

- Did you notice that rounds 3 and 4 were worth more? Did it change how you played or how tense it felt?
- Did 4 rounds of 4 questions feel like the right amount before the final?
- Did RoundEnd (4 s) feel like a breath or a wait?

### 7. The final race (8 s per true or false statement)

- ★ Last place, did you believe you could still win? Leader, did you feel your lead was safe or under threat?
- Was it clear why some started ahead?
- Was 8 s per statement right? Was the race too long, too short, or right (up to 12 statements)?
- Was it the climax of the night, or did it feel like a separate small game?

### 8. Results and the poll

- Did the winner moment feel big enough? Did anyone else get a moment (best answer, most attacks, biggest comeback)? Should they?
- 20 s of Results: too long or too short? What did you want to see there that wasn't there?
- Did going back to the Lobby and pressing Ready again for the next game feel easy?

### 9. The hosts (Konstantin and Ilja)

- ★ Did you read the bubbles? When did you not have time to?
- Which line do you remember? Which one fell flat?
- One line per question at most: too few, right, or too many?
- Did a bubble ever cover something you needed to see, or seem to hold things up? (Pillar 4)
- Do the two cubes at the desk work for now, or do they need faces before the next game night?
- Would you rather hear the hosts out loud than read them?

### 10. The TV: set, guests, look

- From where you sat, could you read the question, the answers, the scores and the cards over the guests? Which one not?
- Could you tell the answer colors apart (A red, B blue, C yellow, D green)? Did you use the color or the letter?
- Did the set and the capsule guests feel like a show you were in, or just a background?
- Was any camera cut confusing or slow?
- Did the board game look (cards, tiles, stamps) feel like a family game night?

### 11. The phone

- ★ Was there a tap that did nothing, a screen you did not understand, or a moment you did not know what to do?
- Did your phone lock, lose the connection, or reload? Did you get back in, and how long did it take?
- Was the text big enough? Did anything feel slow?
- Was the phone fun to use, or just a remote?

### 12. Sound

- Did the music fit? Was it too loud for talking? Did it get repetitive over the evening?
- What moments felt too quiet? Where should there be a sound (a correct answer, an attack, the race)?

### 13. Pauses and problems

- (If the pause menu or a restart was used) Did the pause break the mood? Did the game pick up well after it?
- Did anything look broken, freeze, or need a restart?

### 14. The evening as a whole

- ★ What was the moment everyone reacted together (laughing, shouting, groaning)? What caused it?
- When was the room quietest? Was anyone checked out, and when?
- Did you talk to each other during the game, or was everyone on their own phone?
- Was it a game you play **with** the others, or **next to** them?
- Who in your family would love this, and who would struggle? Why?

## Part C: one person at a time

| Who | Questions |
| --- | --- |
| Last place | At what point did you stop believing you could win? What would have kept you in? Did the head start in the race give you hope or feel too small? |
| The winner | Did the win feel earned or lucky? Did you feel targeted? Was that fair? |
| Most attacked | How did it feel to be hit again and again? Funny, annoying, unfair? What would make it better: a shield, revenge, a limit? |
| Least confident with phones or quizzes | What was hard at any point? What would you change so your parents or grandparents could play? |
| The observer (not playing) | When did players look bored or confused? Was the TV fun to watch without a phone? What did the players not notice that you saw? |
| Konstantin and Ilja, if present | Do the lines sound like you? Which lines would you never say? Give us three lines you would say. |

---

## Part D: close

- ★ If we could change **one thing** before the next game night, what should it be?
- ★ What one thing must we **not** change?
- What was missing that you expected from a party quiz?
- What would make you be the one who asks "can we play it again?"
- How would you describe the game to a friend in one sentence?
